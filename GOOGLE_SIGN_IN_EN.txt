GOOGLE SIGN-IN — LOWTUBE 0.5
This build supports the official Google OAuth desktop flow in your browser.
It has no registered OAuth client ID bundled. A one-time Google Cloud setup
is required before the Sign in with Google button can complete a sign-in.

1. Open https://console.cloud.google.com/ and create/select your project.
2. APIs & Services > Library: enable YouTube Data API v3.
3. Open Google Auth Platform. Configure Branding (app name and your contact
   email) and Audience. For personal testing choose External / Testing and
   add your Google email to Test users. Workspace policies may restrict this.
4. Data Access: add https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly .
5. Clients > Create client > Desktop app. Download the client JSON.
   Do not choose Web application, Android, or a service-account key.
6. In LowTube: Account > Import OAuth client JSON. Select that file.
7. Click Sign in with Google. Choose your account on accounts.google.com
   and grant YouTube read access. Return to LowTube after the browser callback.
8. Open Google subscriptions in the left menu. It reads your subscribed
   channels via the official API, then obtains their recent public videos.
   All subscription pages are read; up to 3000 recent videos are displayed.
   Local subscriptions are independent and are not overwritten.

Testing projects normally require signing in again after seven days.
API disabled, quota limits, administrator restrictions or a revoked grant
can prevent access. Complete your own project setup; do not use someone
else's application identity. Wider distribution needs the applicable Google
verification and publishing steps; this build is not Google-verified.

OAuth does not provide personal Home recommendations, watch history, or
media download URLs. Recommendations remain topic-based; Local history is
this app's history. Existing experimental YouTube history / authenticated
media remain under Account > Firefox / cookies settings. OAuth tokens are
never passed to yt-dlp, mpv, the public video feeds or thumbnail requests.

Google access/refresh tokens are encrypted for your Windows user with DPAPI
in %LOCALAPPDATA%\LowTube\google-session.bin. Client configuration is in
google-client.json. Passwords are entered only on Google's website.
Disconnect removes the local token. Revoke Google access also withdraws
the Google authorization for this OAuth client; subscriptions are unchanged.
You can also manage grants at https://myaccount.google.com/connections .
Closing the dialog or Cancel sign-in stops waiting; timeout is five minutes.

The complete browser-to-Google flow requires your registered client and
account and has not been live-tested with this build. Local callback/state,
PKCE, cancellation and C# compilation have been tested. See VALIDATION.txt.
